ABOUT NOTPROVIDED

NotProvided is a father-and-son alternative pop band built around a shared passion for music. What started as simple improvisation sessions together eventually developed into writing, recording, and releasing original songs.

Combining personal songwriting, atmospheric sounds, and a variety of musical influences, NotProvided continues to develop its own sound with every release. From the early beginnings of Silhouette to newer releases like Oceans and Magnet, the band continues to explore new ideas and creative directions.

At its core, NotProvided is about creating music together and sharing that music with others.
